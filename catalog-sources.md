# Tyler Black catalog provenance

Checked **November 15, 2026**. The 2026 addition contains **49 versions** of **2026 Topps Update #US138 Tyler Black, Milwaukee Brewers**: the base card and 48 parallels or variations. These are checklist-documented records. They are not an assertion that all 49 have been physically sighted, and no collection ownership is implied.

The existing embedded catalog contained 1,479 records with IDs 1–1479 when this file was added. New IDs are fixed at 2001–2049. Do not renumber them: saved ownership and notes refer to these IDs.

## Sources and confidence

1. **Manufacturer checklist, retailer-hosted copy:** [Topps 2026 Update checklist PDF](https://www.yocardo.com/WebRoot/Store28/Shops/91062111/6AB0/D075/2373/F2D5/7C0D/0A0C/6D0F/9144/2026_Topps_Update_Series_Baseball_Checklist.pdf). It explicitly identifies Tyler Black #US138 in the base set on PDF page 3 and in Veterans II Golden Mirror Variation on PDF page 10. This document establishes the player, product, card number, and named image variation; it does not independently document a sighted copy of every color parallel.
2. **Player-specific version rows:** [BreakNinja's 2026 Update Milwaukee checklist](https://www.breakninja.com/baseball/2026-Topps-Update-Series-Baseball-Checklist-Milwaukee-Brewers.php) explicitly lists Tyler Black #US138 for all 49 imported rows. This product/team page is the primary `sourceUrl`, because the separately retrieved player-only page was stale and did not yet include 2026. No generic color-by-finish combinations were generated.
3. **Parallel names and serial denominators:** [Checklist Insider's 2026 Update guide](https://www.checklistinsider.com/2026-topps-update-series-baseball) supplies the full parallel specification. In particular, its Hanger Exclusive Parallels list documents Green Diamante /99, Gold Diamante /50, Orange Diamante /25, Black Diamante /10, and Red Diamante /5, which BreakNinja leaves blank. [TCDB's related sets](https://www.tcdb.com/Inserts.cfm/sid/686684/2026-Topps-Update-Stars-of-MLB) independently corroborate those numbered sets.
4. **Manufacturer odds:** [Topps' official Update odds PDF](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0662/9749/5709/files/2026_Topps_Baseball_Updates_Series_Odds.pdf?v=1790610141) documents product parallels and cautions that subject inclusion does not guarantee every parallel or variation. It is not a serial-number checklist. The [official product page](https://www.topps.com/pages/topps-updates-baseball) lists the October 14, 2026 release.

For these reasons, each new record has `verification: "checklist"`. A later photograph or reliable sighting can support a stronger evidence status without changing its identity or ownership.

### Naming decisions

- The product name follows the existing `2024 Topps Update` convention. Source names that append "Series Baseball" refer to the same product.
- "Base Veterans" is removed from the variant field; Tyler is a veteran, and this is not a 2026 rookie card.
- BreakNinja's "Aqua Foil" is normalized to **Aqua Rainbow Foil**, matching the manufacturer odds and keeping it distinct from Aqua Holo Foil.
- Rainbow and Holo color names retain **Foil** consistently. "Cherry Blossom" becomes **Cherry Blossom Foil**. Each printing-plate color is one row, using singular **Printing Plate**.
- **Golden Mirror Variation** is the canonical name. Its print run remains empty; third-party production estimates are not imported as serial denominators.

## Coverage limits

No other 2026 Tyler Black product was verified during this research. That is a limit of this update, not proof that no obscure or minor-league issue exists. The [published 2026 Topps Chrome Update checklist](https://www.checklistinsider.com/2026-topps-chrome-update-series-baseball) had no Tyler Black entry when checked; it must not be populated by copying the paper Update rainbow. Announced products and unverified marketplace claims require their own evidence before adding checklist records.

Some marketplace listings mislabel #US138 as a rookie. The manufacturer places Tyler in its veteran checklist; seller flags do not override that classification. Likewise, estimated quantities of unnumbered parallels are not official serial-number runs.

## Proofs and other special issues

A **manufacturer-issued proof** is distinct from a custom or fantasy card. [Leaf's release archive](https://www.leaftradingcards.com/releases?0e84ef2b_page=36) describes 2021 Metal Draft Baseball Jumbo boxes as including an autographed 1/1 slabbed proof. Proof status therefore does not imply a fake card or an unsigned card. Licensing and issue type are separate attributes.

Track Pre-Production Proofs, prototypes/test issues, custom/fantasy cards, and unverified additions explicitly. Keep their holdings accessible while excluding them from the standard-checklist completion denominator unless the collector deliberately changes the scope. Never infer a full proof rainbow from an ordinary release's color list.

For each special issue, preserve its actual year, product, subset, card number, color, finish, signed/unsigned status, serial mark, source or photo, and slab/certification reference where available. Link it to the related standard card only when that relationship is known. Deduplicate on the full identity, since card numbers such as `BA-TB1` recur across Leaf products. Preserve existing ownership and notes when reviewing an uncertain classification.
